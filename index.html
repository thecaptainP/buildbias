About

AI bias refers to systematic, repeatable errors in a computer system that create unfair outcomes. This usually happens when an algorithm privileges one category of data or people over another. Understanding AI bias requires looking at how it happens, how it shows up, and why it is so difficult to eliminate.

An AI model learns patterns from data. If those patterns are flawed, the AI copies and amplifies those flaws.
